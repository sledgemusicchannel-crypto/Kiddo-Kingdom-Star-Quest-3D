Apex Mobility System ("we," "our," or "us") respects the privacy of children, parents, and players. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Kiddo Kingdom: Star Quest 3D.

1. Information We Collect and Local Storage

We do not collect, transmit, rent, or sell personal information to external servers or third-party data brokers.

  • Player Names & Game Progress: If you type a custom player name or pick a hero for single-player or pass-and-play sessions, that name—along with your stars, unlocked worlds, and local scores—is saved only on your device via local browser/WebView storage.
  • No Account or Sign-In Required: Players can start immediately without creating an account or sharing an email address, phone number, photo, or location.

2. Advertising & Google AdMob

Kiddo Kingdom: Star Quest 3D is free to play and supported by optional, family-safe rewarded advertisements served through Google AdMob.

  • Child-Directed Treatment: Ad requests are configured with child-directed treatment enabled (tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true) and restricted to General Audience (G-rated) ads.
  • No Personalized Ads: Behavioral advertising and interest-based ad targeting are disabled for child-directed traffic in accordance with Google Play Families policies and COPPA.
  • Google's Privacy Policy: Learn more about how Google handles data in ad services at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Offline Play & Device Permissions

Gameplay works offline on your device. Internet access is used only when loading optional rewarded video ads. The game does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or GPS location.

4. Parental Rights & Deleting Local Data

Parents or guardians can delete all locally stored player names and game progress at any time by clearing the app's storage/cache in Android settings or uninstalling the game.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. The effective date at the top shows when the latest version took effect.
